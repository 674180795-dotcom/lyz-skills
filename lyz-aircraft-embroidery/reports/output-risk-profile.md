# 输出风险

- Aircraft model contamination: canards or tailplanes transferred from another model.
- Material resembles print/PVC instead of directional embroidery.
- Background or clipped wings conceal identification features.
- Same palette drifts between rim, canopy and field color roles.
- Compiler fixtures mistaken for actual image quality or model baseline evidence.

修复顺序：型号→材质→图地→配色；真实图像检查见live-generation.json。
